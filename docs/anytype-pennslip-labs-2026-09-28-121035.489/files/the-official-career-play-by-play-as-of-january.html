application strategy

doing applications in waves can help me avoid job-hunting burn-out while also giving myself time and space to do other things in my life.

  • starting with a week of focused applications
  • then following with a resting period, skill building, or resume tweaking

make sure to follow the to-do list within 📋Job Application Tracker

concise plan job application cycle

  1. Apply in Wave 1 for 1–2 weeks to the easiest entry roles. Aim for 20–30 targeted applications during this wave.
  1. Take a Rest + Skill Week for 1–2 weeks to recharge, practice interview questions, lightly improve your resume or GitHub, or add a small feature to a project if you feel like it.
  1. Apply in Wave 2 for 1–2 weeks to the slightly more technical roles
  1. Repeat the cycle of Wave → Rest until you land a job, keeping the rhythm consistent without tying yourself to rigid deadlines.
