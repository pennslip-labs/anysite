# how to sequence each track   
in order to make meaningful progress within each stream given i'm also doing this while working, I will need to dedicate a couple weeks at a time to each track.   
> though with job applications, I will slip in applications here and there between and during other tracks when I see a job posting of interest.    

thus it will be a cycle as follows:   
  1. track A ~ skill building → 4-6 weeks   
  2. rest week   
  3. track b ~ income streams → 4-6 weeks   
  4. rest week   
  5. here, I bring back attention to which ever needs more attention between track A and B → 2-3 weeks   
  6. rest for half a week   
  7. track C ~ job search → 4-6 weeks   
  8. rest week   
the idea here is to ensure I'm making progress in each avenue while giving myself space to rest and focus on other parts of my life that don't involve career building
