### What is it for?   
Idea is to solve two issues:   
  - data autonomy   
  - care focused design and practicality   
A lot of period apps on the market have many features locked behind a paywall. making many of them virtually useless to people that may not be able to afford a subscription service.   
Additionally, those that are free tend to be quite limited in capability. As well as have invasive data collection.   
Within the app, I aim to have a social component to allow people to connect with one another and share knowledge and experiences.   
### Goals   
Make an app thats easy to use and friendly to those who need a little extra support during their menstrual cycle.   
Providing a safe zone to help in going through the rough times, and connect with others that may be going through the same   
### Measure of success…   
Success would mean having people actively using our app, and being able to receive genuine and impact-full support. Both through recommendations, and socially with people they may meet.
